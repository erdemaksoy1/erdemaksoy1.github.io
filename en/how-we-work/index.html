How We Work

A planned, transparent and controlled process from first survey to handover.

A planned path from first survey to handover

We approach every project with careful planning, transparent communication and controlled installation. From the first survey to handover, we make the whole process clear, traceable and manageable for you.

  1. 01

    01. Project Review

    We assess the scope of your project, your expectations and current conditions together to clarify your needs, creating the right roadmap from the start.

    Outcome of this step: Project roadmap

  2. 02

    02. Site Survey

    We inspect the installation area on site to determine measurements, site conditions and potential requirements, so that surprises are avoided before work begins.

    Outcome of this step: Site findings

  3. 03

    03. Quantity Take-off

    We calculate the labour and material quantities required for the project scope, making the quotation clearer and more predictable.

    Outcome of this step: Labour and material quantities

  4. 04

    04. Quotation

    Based on the agreed scope, we provide a clear quotation that sets out your needs and every cost item transparently.

    Outcome of this step: Itemised quotation

  5. 05

    05. Material Supply

    Within the approved scope, we manage the procurement of the required materials and complete the preparations needed for installation to proceed as planned.

    Outcome of this step: Procurement plan

  6. 06

    06. Installation

    We carry out the planned scope in line with your project’s technical requirements and site conditions, managing labour, materials and site coordination together.

    Outcome of this step: Coordinated installation

  7. 07

    07. Testing & Handover

    We inspect the completed installation, carry out the necessary tests and final checks, and hand over the project complete.

    Outcome of this step: Complete handover

From quotation to handover, we follow your project’s needs on your behalf.
